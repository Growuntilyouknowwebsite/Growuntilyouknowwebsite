Fresh Encouragement Every Week

This Week in the Grow Until You Know™ Community

Simple ideas to nourish the body, encourage the mind, and keep growing—one choice at a time.

🥤

Healthy Beverage of the Week

Cucumber, Lemon & Mint Water

Add fresh cucumber, lemon slices, and mint to cold water for a colorful, refreshing way to make hydration feel special.

Try it: Prepare a pitcher and chill it before serving.

🥗

Healthy Meal of the Week

Colorful Build-Your-Own Bowl

Layer brown rice or quinoa with leafy greens, roasted vegetables, beans, and a favorite protein for a flexible family meal.

Grow together: Let each family member choose a colorful topping.

🍎

Healthy Fruit of the Week

The Crisp, Colorful Apple

Apples are portable, naturally sweet, and easy to pair with breakfast, lunch, or an afternoon snack.

Explore: Compare two varieties and describe their taste and texture.

🚶🏽‍♀️

Healthy Exercise of the Week

The Grow-and-Go Family Walk

Take a comfortable 15-to-20-minute walk and notice five interesting things in your surroundings.

Make it yours: Choose a pace and duration that feel safe for you.
